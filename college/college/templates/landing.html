About Us

A Legacy of Excellence

NSS College of Engineering, Palakkad, established in 1960, stands as one of Kerala’s premier engineering institutions. The college has a rich tradition of nurturing future engineers through innovation, technology, and holistic education.

  • 🏛️ Established in 1960
  • 🎓 Affiliated to APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University
  • 🌏 Accredited by NBA & NAAC
